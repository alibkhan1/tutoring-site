Club life

Past events

Big traditions, service opportunities, and the projects our members help bring to life.

Competition

Middle School Math Competition

We invite all of our feeder middle schools to BVSW for a friendly math competition with real trophies, treats for everyone, and a fun first look at the high-school math community.

Community night

STEM Fair

We partner with Science NHS to plan hands-on projects for families and friends to explore in the cafeteria. Past projects have ranged from elephant's toothpaste to mini pancakes, marshmallow builds, robotics, and homemade ice cream.

All-day tradition

Pi Day

Math classes rotate through the lunchroom every hour for games, prizes, class point competitions, a pie-eating contest, jar guessing challenges, and more. Our members plan the activities, buy supplies, set up the space, and keep the day running.

Once a month

General Meetings

Meetings combine quick club updates with activities ranging from math-field professionals speaking about their work to member-led group games.

Coming soon

T-Shirt Design Contest

At the start of each year, members submit designs and earn bonus points for winning or placing as a runner-up. The winning design decides what we wear to every event for the rest of the year.

Coming soon

Math Posters

Members create clever math problems, brain teasers, and jokes to decorate the math hallways and give everyone something worth stopping to solve.
