Our community

About us

BVSW Math NHS is for students who want to go a little further with math — showing up for the school's STEM events, building creative projects, and running a few fun events of our own. We create welcoming ways to participate, serve our school, and help more students see math as something worth exploring.

Membership is built around points so everyone gets credit for what they put in: showing up, volunteering at events, creating something useful, and raising interest in math at BVSW and our feeder schools.
